Solar Educational and Care Guides

Learn how proper maintenance maximizes solar longevity, safety, and financial return.

1. Why Tap Water and Soap Damage Solar Glass

It can be tempting to spray rooftop panels with a garden hose or soapy bucket. However, untreated tap water contains calcium and magnesium that cause permanent hard-water staining.

Soap leaves a thin sticky film on panel surfaces. While clean initially, this film attracts pollen, dust, and vehicle exhaust, causing your panels to get dirty twice as fast as panels cleaned with pure deionized water.

2. Understanding Hotspots and Micro-Cracks

Accumulated debris or dried bird droppings do more than look bad. They cause physical electrical hazards within your array through cell overloading.

When a section of a panel is blocked from light, it stops producing electricity and becomes an electrical resistance point. Surrounding cells force energy through this resistance, generating extreme heat points. Hotspots can crack panel glass, burn internal solder joints, and cause permanent diode failures.

3. Bird Pest Prevention: Protecting Under-Panel Wiring

The gap underneath solar arrays offers shelter for pigeons and pests. Nesting activity creates severe long-term maintenance issues.

  • Wire Damage: Rodents and birds nest near warm conduits and chew through critical insulation cabling.
  • Fire Hazards: Dry leaves, twigs, and nesting materials packed beneath hot panels create fire hazards.
  • Corrosive Droppings: Acidic bird waste corrodes aluminum mounting frames and copper wiring connections.
